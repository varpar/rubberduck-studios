# Pond asset credits

The following Sketchfab models are used in the pond scene under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). The tree, water lily, and log were simplified; embedded textures were converted to WebP; and the tree and cattail material workflow was converted to current glTF PBR.
